# Name and trademark screening: "Vardena"

Checked 2026-10-12 in public registers. A first screening, not a legal opinion: it does not cover unregistered rights, and TMview states that it has no legal effect. Have a trademark attorney look at the similar marks below before money goes into the name.

## Company name (Bolagsverket)
- No company named Vardena is registered. Searches for Wardena, Vardana, Verdena and Vardenia also gave no hit.
- The company behind the site, 559602-6616, is registered as **Grundbulten 163259 AB** (shelf company, registered 2026-09-21, address c/o Fortnox Starta Företag AB, Växjö). Its only case is the formation, 536253/2026; no change of name has been filed. Until Bolagsverket registers the change, Vardena is a brand, not the company's name.
- Similar registered names: Ardena Sweden AB (556565-7094, research), Gardena Germany AB and Gardena Norden (Husqvarna group).

## Trademarks (TMview: PRV, EUIPO, WIPO)
Exact search across all offices, and a fuzzy search limited to PRV, EUIPO and WIPO registrations covering Sweden or the EU (110 results).

- No identical mark in classes 35 or 42, and none at all at PRV or as an EU trade mark.
- Identical, other field: VARDENA, international registration 1323387 (figurative, class 25, clothing; Federico Simone, Italy; basic Italian mark 2016000070832). Its term ran to 2026-07-29 and it still showed as registered.
- Close, same field: **ARDENA**, EU trade mark 019341467, word mark, classes 9 and 42 (software, software development, software as a service, excluding education), Meet Intelligent Innovations, Croatia, registered 2026-08-08. One letter from Vardena, in the class an IT consultancy would file in. An opposition against it for likelihood of confusion was filed and withdrawn in 2026.
- Close, well known: **GARDENA**, Husqvarna AB, many Swedish, EU and international registrations, several covering classes 35 and 42 (for example EUTM 018384219 and 018162155, IR 815929).
- Further off: VARENA (IR 1030076, classes 35, 36, 37, 42, shopping centres), Ardena (IR 1358626, classes 42 and 45, pharmaceutical services), Kardena (EUTM 018808361, classes 9 and 10), NARDENA (EUTM 019049129 and 019339419, includes class 35), verdena (EUTM 019066813, class 3).
- Many pharmaceutical marks begin with Varden-, after the substance vardenafil (VARDENARISTO, EUTM 017204728, class 5). No conflict of field, but the association exists.
