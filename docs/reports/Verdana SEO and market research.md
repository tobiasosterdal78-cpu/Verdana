# Lead with NAV, prices and precision

Verdana should launch as the Swedish Business Central consultancy that publishes what its rivals blur: dated Microsoft prices, NAV deadlines by version and a visible delivery method. Both language versions should be built around the product name, because **"business central" is the only commercial query with measurable Swedish demand**, with a five-year Google Trends index of **42** that reached **59.8** over the last year, while every service modifier sits at the Trends floor ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=SE&q=aff%C3%A4rssystem,erp%20system,ekonomisystem,business%20central,navision&hl=sv)). The NAV cluster comes first: **NAV 2017 extended support ends in January 2027 and NAV 2018 in January 2028** ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/dynamics-nav-2017), [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/dynamics-nav-2018)), Swedish upgrade searches return mostly English pages ([Bing](https://www.bing.com/search?q=uppgradera+navision+till+business+central&setlang=sv&cc=SE)), and none of the 15 Swedish competitor sites checked organizes its migration content by NAV version ([NAB](https://nabsolutions.com/se/erp/navision-microsoft-dynamics-nav-och-vagen-vidare-till-business-central/), [BrightCom](https://www.brightcom.se/artiklar/uppgradera-dynamics-nav)). The Swedish site at `/sv` should use Swedish wording for price, licensing, implementation and ERP selection but the English lifecycle phrases Swedes actually type, while the English site at `/` should target Sweden-qualified buyer questions such as "how to choose a business central partner in sweden" and avoid "business central consultant", which Google completes with jobs and salary ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=how%20to%20choose%20a%20business%20central%20partner), [Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=chrome&hl=en&gl=se&q=business%20central%20consultant)). Microsoft's own pages settle the facts the market contradicts, **Essentials 764,70 kr, Premium 1 051,40 kr and Team Members 76,50 kr per user and month**, paid yearly, excluding VAT and sold only through CSP partners ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/sv-se/dynamics-365/products/business-central/pricing), [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/deployment/licensing)), against competitor figures that range from 600 to 950 kr. Technically the site needs one root layout per language, self-referencing canonicals with reciprocal `en`/`sv` hreflang and `x-default` on the English URL, a sitemap of canonical URLs only, and a WebSite-plus-Organization graph that omits LocalBusiness and ProfessionalService, and FAQ markup too, since Google stopped showing FAQ rich results on **7 May 2026** ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)). The name is the lasting risk, since "verdana" means the Microsoft font in English and Verdana Fond & Försäkring AB in Swedish autocomplete, so every title, the site name and the Organization markup must pair it with "Business Central", and a Bolagsverket and PRV/EUIPO check must come before launch. Phosphor icons suit the brief as long as no Microsoft product glyph or sparkle stands in for Microsoft's marks, and the order of work runs from launch blockers to the money pages, NAV cluster and pricing before January 2027, then decision guides, then Swedish industry and help content.

## Fifteen Swedish rivals leave the independent consultancy lane open

No one publishes a credible Swedish ERP market share. Even the comparison site Affärssystemguiden says no public measurement exists and falls back on vendor counts such as Fortnox's **612,000 subscription customers** ([Affärssystemguiden](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq)). The market is segmented instead: Fortnox and Spiris serve micro firms, Monitor claims **46% of Swedish manufacturing** in Publikator's 2025 survey as reported by Monitor itself ([Monitor ERP](https://www.monitorerp.com/sv/kunskapsbank/aktuellt-fran-monitor/monitor-erp-starker-sin-position-som-marknadsledare-i-sverige-med-landets-mest-nojda-kunder/)), and Business Central sits between them, positioned for roughly **5–500 employees** ([Affärssystemguiden](https://affarssystemguiden.se/jamfor/dynamics-365-business-central-vs-fortnox)). A partner-affiliated buyer's guide puts the fit at **5–300 users** and quotes implementation bands from 150,000–300,000 kr over 8–14 weeks for small projects to 500,000–1.5 million kr or more over 24–40 weeks for large ones ([D365.SE](https://d365.se/businesscentral/)). Sweden had about **41,000 small and 7,200 medium-sized companies** in 2023 ([Svenskt Näringsliv](https://www.svensktnaringsliv.se/sme/)), and companies replace their ERP every **12–15 years** ([HerbertNathan & Co](https://herbertnathan.com/erp/konkurrensen-inom-affarssystem-leder-till-utslagning/)); dividing one figure by the other gives roughly 3,200–4,000 replacement decisions a year in that band. That number is an inference, not a measurement, but it sets the economics of the site: few buyers, each making a decade-long commitment, which rewards trust and specificity over traffic. Sweden also had **8,160 foreign-controlled enterprises with 837,679 employees** in 2023, led by US, German and Norwegian owners ([Tillväxtanalys](https://www.tillvaxtanalys.se/en-GB/publikationer/statistik/statistikserien/utlandskaforetag2023.128258.html)), a natural English-speaking buyer group that supports English as the default language, and **72%** of Swedish firms with ten or more employees already buy cloud services ([SCB](https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/forskning-och-det-digitala-samhallet/det-digitala-samhallet/it-anvandning-i-foretag/)), which suits a cloud-first product.

The competitor review covered 15 Swedish sites on 11 October 2026: the eight named rivals (NAB, Fellowmind, Modia, Exsitec, BrightCom, InBiz, Evidi and Sherpas) plus Navet, Enqore, Effekt, Yellow Solution, Basecloud, CGI and Azets. The field is consolidating into groups. BrightCom's footer identifies it as part of Exsitec ([BrightCom](https://www.brightcom.se/)), Goodfellows Business is now Modia ([Modia](https://modia.se/ett-nytt-kapitel)), NaviPro is now Evidi ([Evidi](https://www.evidi.com/se/navipro)), Navet carries an Aderian brand bar ([Navet](https://navet.se/)) and Sherpas belongs to Serpentin ([Sherpas](https://sherpas.se/om-oss/)). Almost every site calls itself a partner; only Enqore has a dedicated Business Central-konsult page ([Enqore](https://enqore.se/erp-hos-enqore/business-central/business-central-konsult/)). No site has city pages, a Business Central cost calculator or online booking. NAB's **3,626-word** NAV guide is the deepest migration page in the set and is still not organized by NAV version ([NAB](https://nabsolutions.com/se/erp/navision-microsoft-dynamics-nav-och-vagen-vidare-till-business-central/)), and the only effort figure anyone publishes for a NAV upgrade is BrightCom's offer of upgrades from 40 hours ([BrightCom](https://www.brightcom.se/artiklar/uppgradera-dynamics-nav)).

Prices are where the field is weakest. Microsoft lists Essentials at 764,70 kr ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/sv-se/dynamics-365/products/business-central/pricing)), yet NAB shows **918 kr on a one-month term and 765 kr on a 12–36-month term** ([NAB](https://nabsolutions.com/se/om-nab-solutions/priser/)), Navet 884 kr ([Navet](https://navet.se/business-central/hur-mycket-kostar-en-business-central-licens-i-sverige)), BrightCom 950 kr ([BrightCom](https://www.brightcom.se/dynamics-business-central/priser/)) and Modia a range of 600–900 kr ([Modia](https://modia.se/kunskapsbank/vad-kostar-ett-affarssystem)), while Fellowmind starts from Microsoft's 764,70 kr and adds fixed packages of 79,000 and 99,000 kr and support at 295 kr per user and month ([Fellowmind](https://www.fellowmind.com/sv-se/losningar/business-applications-erp/dynamics-365-business-central/ready-set-bc/)). Arithmetic explains most of the spread. NAB's one-month prices sit about 20% above its long-term prices for all three licence types (exactly 20% for Essentials), and Navet's figures equal Microsoft's dollar prices converted at about 11.05 kr instead of the roughly 9.56 kr implied by Microsoft's Swedish list. Both readings are inferences from the published numbers: Microsoft's Swedish page shows only the annual-commitment price, and the research found no Microsoft source for a monthly-term premium. A buyer who searches "vad kostar business central", a query on which NAB, Fellowmind and Cosmo Consult buy Bing ads ([Bing](https://www.bing.com/search?q=vad+kostar+business+central&setlang=sv&cc=SE)), therefore meets five different answers. A dated page that reconciles them with Microsoft's list is Verdana's strongest commercial content and its cheapest trust signal.

Technical quality is uneven enough to be a positioning lever. Modia serves the same 2 KB HTML shell for every URL, so page-specific titles and copy exist only after JavaScript runs ([Modia](https://modia.se/)); Sherpas gives its home page a generic Home title and has no working sitemap ([Sherpas](https://sherpas.se/)); Fellowmind's Swedish home page carries an English title and H1 ([Fellowmind](https://www.fellowmind.com/sv-se/)); Effekt's Swedish page canonicalizes to English ([Effekt](https://effekt.se/)); InBiz declares `lang="en-US"` and still serves default WordPress pages ([InBiz](https://inbiz.se/)); and Navet takes 2.2 s to deliver its first byte ([Navet](https://navet.se/)). In unthrottled lab runs, NAB, Fellowmind and Sherpas each transferred more than 7 MB on mobile, Exsitec made 137 requests and needed 2.4 s for its largest paint, and Evidi needed 3.5 s ([Exsitec](https://www.exsitec.se/), [Evidi](https://www.evidi.com/se/)). InBiz still advertises Silver status and Sherpas Gold Partner status ([InBiz](https://inbiz.se/microsoft-partner/), [Sherpas](https://sherpas.se/nyheter/)), tiers Microsoft retired when its old partner network ended on 3 October 2022 ([RCP](https://rcpmag.com/articles/2022/10/07/qa-microsoft-cloud-partner-program-launch.aspx)).

Three positions are therefore unoccupied: an independent voice focused only on Business Central, full transparency on prices, method and support terms, and technical excellence combined with deep product content. Verdana should claim all three and call itself a consultancy (konsult), not a partner, until Partner Center shows a designation; the Business Applications designation requires a partner capability score of at least 70 out of 100 ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/membership/solutions-partner-business)). With no references yet, trust has to come from verifiable specifics. The design research proposes a fact ledger of dated, sourced Microsoft facts in place of a logo strip, a published method with deliverables and decision gates, a first-meeting promise, named people with checkable certifications, company registration facts and an honest statement of when Business Central is the wrong choice, and it rules out logo strips, testimonials, counters, unauthorized partner badges, stock photos and superlatives. Nielsen Norman Group's research supports that approach: upfront disclosure and current, comprehensive content build trust, while stock photos of generic people are ignored ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/), [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/)). Positioning should lead with distribution, e-commerce and service businesses rather than manufacturing. Monitor dominates that segment, and the survey Monitor cites gives "Microsoft Dynamics" a manufacturing NPS of **−34**, a label that does not separate Business Central from legacy AX and NAV installations ([Monitor ERP](https://www.monitorerp.com/sv/kunskapsbank/aktuellt-fran-monitor/monitor-erp-starker-sin-position-som-marknadsledare-i-sverige-med-landets-mest-nojda-kunder/)). That figure belongs off the site, but it explains why the manufacturing page needs proof of depth before it ships.

## Swedish demand is thin, specific and half in English

Google Trends registers steady Swedish interest in only three terms: **"business central"** (five-year index **42**, **59.8** over the last twelve months), **"affärssystem" (24)** and **"erp system" (14)**, with "ekonomisystem" at 2 and "navision" at 1 ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=SE&q=aff%C3%A4rssystem,erp%20system,ekonomisystem,business%20central,navision&hl=sv)). Every service modifier tested, from "business central partner" through "pris", "konsult" and "support" to "integration", averages zero over five years ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=SE&q=business%20central%20partner,business%20central%20pris,business%20central%20konsult,business%20central%20support,business%20central%20integration&hl=sv)). No paid keyword tool was used, so the priorities below rest on evidence tiers (Trends data, autocomplete presence, advertisers and the results Bing shows), not on search volumes. The bare product name is navigational, completing to login, status and api ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central)), and Microsoft will own it; the winnable form is the bridge between product and category, such as "business central sverige" or "vad är business central" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central%20sverige)).

Swedes type lifecycle and migration queries in English. Google recognizes "navision end of life" along with version forms such as "nav 2017 end of life" and "navision 2018 end of life", yet returns nothing for "navision support upphör" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=navision%20end%20of%20life), [Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=navision%20support%20upph%C3%B6r)). It rewrites "migrera nav till business central" as "migrate nav to business central" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=migrera%20nav%20till%20business%20central)), and "business central eller" returns nothing, so comparison titles use "vs" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central%20eller)). In Swedish Trends, **"business central navision" rose 450%**, while "shopify business central" and "affärssystem för tillverkning" are breakout queries ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%205-y&geo=SE&q=aff%C3%A4rssystem,erp%20system,ekonomisystem,business%20central,navision&hl=sv)). Industry needs are phrased "affärssystem för …", and "business central för tillverkning" returns nothing ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=aff%C3%A4rssystem%20f%C3%B6r), [Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central%20f%C3%B6r%20tillverkning)). Job seekers dilute consultant intent, since "business central konsult lön" is the second suggestion for "business central konsult" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central%20konsult)). The Swedish homepage should therefore own "Business Central-konsulter", the consultants page should own hiring language ("hyra konsult"), and "partner" belongs in a neutral partner-choice guide until Verdana's status is verified.

The English picture inverts the Swedish one. Global head terms belong to Microsoft, directories and recruiters, and Google's top completions for "business central consultant" are jobs and salary, at relevance scores of 1251 and 1250 ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=chrome&hl=en&gl=se&q=business%20central%20consultant)). Interest is growing regardless: worldwide, the related queries "business central pricing" and "business central partner" rose **110%** and **60%** over the year ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%2012-m&q=business%20central)). In Sweden, Bing's top ten results for the English query "business central partner" were all Swedish-language or Swedish-market pages ([Bing](https://www.bing.com/search?q=business%20central%20partner&cc=SE&setlang=en)), and Google completes Sweden-specific buyer questions such as "how to choose a business central partner in sweden" and "how much does business central cost in sweden" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=how%20to%20choose%20a%20business%20central%20partner), [Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=how%20much%20does%20business%20central%20cost)). An English page anchored in Sweden competes against pages its readers cannot use, whereas a translated generic page would compete against Microsoft and global content marketers. Regional interest peaks in Stockholm County (100), Västra Götaland (86) and Skåne (84) ([Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?date=today%2012-m&geo=SE&q=business%20central)), yet city variants return no Swedish suggestions ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=business%20central%20partner%20stockholm)), so cities belong in contact details and Organization data rather than in landing pages. ## Each page pair shares a topic but not its wording

The tables below give every planned page a title, H1 and meta description, with lengths counted by script: all **114 titles and descriptions** fit the 60- and 155-character budgets used throughout the research. Four rules shaped them. Every Business Central service and product page names the product in both title and H1; only the advisory page, the industry pages and the generic ERP articles lead with "ERP" or "affärssystem", because that is how those queries are phrased. Titles end in "| Verdana" so the brand sits next to the category in results, and insight titles too long for the suffix use Next.js `title.absolute` and rely on the site name Google displays separately ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names)). H1s carry the keyword even where the design prefers a slogan; a slogan such as "Affärssystem som bär bolaget – inte bromsar det." moves to the lead or an H2, while "Business Central, infört med precision." qualifies as an H1 because it names the product. Wording is localized to each language's query patterns rather than translated: the English NAV guide is titled "How to Migrate from NAV to Business Central", while its Swedish pair, the article already in the repository, becomes "Business Central vs Navision – skillnader och vägval". Both carry one shared guide body, which keeps the pair a true equivalent for hreflang.

English pages use English slugs at the root and Swedish pages keep Swedish slugs under `/sv/`, paired one to one; Google states that localized words in URLs are fine and lists subdirectories as the low-maintenance structure ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)). Slugs are lowercase ASCII with å, ä and ö transliterated to a, a and o, hyphens between words and no years, in line with Google's URL guidance ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/url-structure)) and Swedish competitors' practice. Because nothing is indexed yet and URL keywords are a minor signal, the slugs already wired into the build's route table should stay ([routes.ts](../../src/lib/routes.ts)), with one exception: at the time of writing the build names the Swedish lifecycle article `dynamics-nav-support-upphor`, a phrase with no autocomplete demand, where `navision-end-of-life` matches what Swedes type. The Swedish keyword research proposed a fuller alternative set (`implementering`, `navision-till-business-central`, `integrationer-och-utveckling`, `support-och-optimering`, `om-verdana`); it is defensible but buys little, and whichever set wins must be frozen before the first crawl. Pages without an equivalent, namely the two English-only guides and the Swedish help and industry pages, get a self-canonical and no hreflang, and their language switcher links to the other language's hub without any hreflang annotation. ## A static two-layout build carries the technical SEO

English at `/` and Swedish at `/sv/` is the subdirectory model Google describes as easy to set up and low maintenance ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)). In Next.js 16.4 each language gets its own root layout inside a route group, so that `<html lang="en">` and `<html lang="sv">` render correctly. Moving between root layouts triggers a full page load, which is acceptable for a language switch, and without a top-level layout the English home page must live inside its route group ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/route-groups.md)). Every true pair carries reciprocal `en` and `sv` alternates plus `x-default` pointing to the English URL: Google requires each version to list itself and all others, ignores annotations that do not point back, and accepts language-only codes ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)). The language-only codes are deliberate, because `en-SE` would narrow the English page to English speakers in Sweden. The site must never redirect by IP address or browser language, which Google advises against; the language switcher links to the equivalent page instead ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)). Google treats HTML link tags and sitemap annotations as equivalent ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)), so emitting both is safe only when both come from the same route table, as they do in the current build. The rendered head of a paired page should look like this:

```html
<!-- https://<domain>/services/support -->
<link rel="canonical" href="https://<domain>/services/support">
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en" href="https://<domain>/services/support">
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="sv" href="https://<domain>/sv/tjanster/support">
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://<domain>/services/support">
<!-- /sv/tjanster/support repeats the three alternates; its canonical is its own /sv/ URL -->
```

Canonicals must be absolute, self-referencing and same-language, and a `/sv/` page must never canonicalize to its English pair or the reverse ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls)). Two Next.js behaviors make this error-prone. Metadata objects merge shallowly, so a page that sets `alternates` replaces its parent's object while a page that sets nothing inherits it, which means a canonical defined in a root layout leaks into every child; and `title.template` applies only to child segments ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/generate-metadata.md)). A single `pageMetadata` helper that every page calls, fed from the route table, avoids both problems, and `metadataBase` should come from an environment variable until the production domain is decided.

Structured data has lost most of its visible rewards. **FAQ rich results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026** and Google removed the documentation on 15 June 2026, HowTo documentation went in September 2023, and breadcrumb rich results now show on desktop only ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/updates), [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/search-gallery)). LocalBusiness requires a physical address ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business)), schema.org has deprecated ProfessionalService ([schema.org](https://schema.org/ProfessionalService)), and Google treats reviews that a business controls about itself as self-serving and ineligible for stars ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)). What still earns its place is entity clarity: one home-page graph in which WebSite carries the site name "Verdana" with the alternate name "Verdana Business Central", and Organization carries the legal name, a logo of at least 112 px, a disambiguating description, `knowsAbout`, `areaServed`, `sameAs` links to profiles that exist, and `vatID` plus `iso6523Code` once the company's registration numbers appear on the site ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization), [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names)). The Swedish VAT number is `SE` plus the ten-digit organisationsnummer plus `01`, and the organisationsnummer carries ISO 6523 code `0007` ([Peppol](https://docs.peppol.eu/pracc/codelist/ICD), [Tickstar](https://www.tickstar.com/article/peppol-bis-iso6523-codes)). Articles should use BlogPosting or Article with named Person authors, deep pages BreadcrumbList, and service pages optional Service nodes that earn no rich result. JSON-LD should be serialized with `<` escaped as `\u003c` ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/json-ld.md)). Visible FAQ blocks remain worth writing for readers and answer engines even though their markup no longer earns a rich result.

The sitemap should list only canonical, indexable URLs that return 200, with real `lastModified` dates where content has a true edit date, because Google ignores `priority` and `changefreq` ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap), [Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/01-metadata/sitemap.md)). The robots file should reference the sitemap, keep crawling closed by environment until launch, and allow Googlebot, Bingbot and the AI search and user agents named below. Every public page should be statically prerendered; without Cache Components, `export const dynamic = 'error'` makes a page fail the build if it turns dynamic ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/caching-without-cache-components.md)). Static output also sidesteps a subtle gap: the default `htmlLimitedBots` pattern, which decides which crawlers receive blocking-rendered metadata on dynamic routes, contains no AI crawler such as GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot or Claude-SearchBot ([Next.js source](../../node_modules/next/dist/shared/lib/router/utils/html-bots.js)). Core Web Vitals targets are LCP at or under 2.5 s, INP at or under 200 ms and CLS at or under 0.1 at the 75th percentile ([web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/defining-core-web-vitals-thresholds)). Fonts belong in `next/font`, which preloads them and generates metric-matched fallbacks ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/font.md)), and the hero image should use `preload`, which replaced the deprecated `priority` in Next.js 16 ([Next.js docs](../../node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/02-components/image.md)). Against competitors that ship 7 MB pages, a budget of about 1.5 MB per page puts Verdana measurably ahead.

AI search needs no special files. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode require only an indexed, snippet-eligible page, with Search Console's "Search generative AI" setting left on Include, a control that rolled out worldwide on **31 August 2026** ([Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-controls-website-owners/)); Google states that llms.txt has no effect on Search ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)). The robots file should admit OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)), PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User ([Perplexity](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots)), and Anthropic's Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User ([Anthropic](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler)); whether to admit training crawlers is a business decision, not an SEO requirement. Content that answer engines can quote is definition-first, uses tables and dated facts, and answers questions visibly on the page, which the fact ledger and the NAV and pricing pages do by design. Bing Webmaster Tools, which now reports AI performance ([Bing Webmaster Blog](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview)), should be verified alongside Search Console.

Local and entity signals depend on facts only Verdana can supply. A service-area Google Business Profile needs a real, verifiable base address, which is then hidden, and a service area within about two hours' drive; virtual offices and unstaffed coworking addresses are ineligible ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177)). Name, address, phone, email domain and URL must match across the footer, the Organization markup, the Business Profile, Bing Places, LinkedIn, hitta.se and allabolag.se, which mirrors Bolagsverket. The Swedish e-commerce act (lag 2002:562, 8 §) requires a service provider to show its name, address and email address, plus organisationsnummer and VAT number where applicable, which makes a publishable address a legal question as well as a local-search one ([Riksdagen](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2002-562.html)). A Microsoft Marketplace consulting offer becomes possible only with the Business Applications designation or at least two Business Central deployments in the past 12 months ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/marketplace/certification-policies)).

The build was changing while this report was written. At about 15:50 on 11 October 2026 it already had per-language root layouts, crawling closed until `SITE_INDEXING=on`, a route table that feeds canonicals, hreflang with `x-default` on English and sitemap alternates, and Phosphor icons imported from `/ssr` ([routes.ts](../../src/lib/routes.ts), [seo.ts](../../src/lib/seo.ts), [sitemap.ts](../../src/app/sitemap.ts), [robots.ts](../../src/app/robots.ts)). The checks that snapshot could not settle belong in the launch gate: single-language pages must receive no hreflang, which a route table that assumes every page exists in both languages does not yet express; the sitemap should gain true `lastModified` dates for articles; WebSite needs `alternateName`, Organization needs `legalName`, `sameAs` and `disambiguatingDescription`, and articles need Person authors; FAQPage markup is optional; fonts should move from a CSS import to `next/font`; and the Swedish lifecycle slug should change before the first crawl.

## A font, a fund and Microsoft's marks constrain the name and the icons

The brand name cannot rank on its own. In English, Google's top completion for "verdana" is "font", at relevance 1250 against 650 for "font download" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=chrome&hl=en&gl=se&q=verdana)); "verdana microsoft" completes to the font and its download ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=verdana%20microsoft)); and Bing's results in Sweden for "verdana business central" were all about the typeface, a Dubai property project and the investor Verdane ([Bing](https://www.bing.com/search?q=verdana%20business%20central&cc=SE&setlang=en)). In Swedish, **"verdana fond & försäkring ab" is the fifth suggestion for "verdana "** ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=verdana%20)), Bing suggests "verdane capital" ([Bing Autocomplete](https://api.bing.com/osjson.aspx?query=verdana%20e&mkt=sv-SE)), and "verdana consulting" completes to "verdana sfi consulting" ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=verdana%20consulting)). The compound "verdana business central" returns no relevant completion in Swedish or English Google ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=sv&gl=se&q=verdana%20business%20central), [Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=verdana%20business%20central)): it is uncontested, but it has no demand until Verdana creates it off-search through LinkedIn, events and directory listings.

"Verdana Business Central" should therefore work as the brand wherever search systems look. The site name stays "Verdana", with "Verdana Business Central" as `alternateName` on both WebSite and Organization, which Google's site-name system reads in order of preference ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names)). The Organization's `disambiguatingDescription` should read "Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central consultancy based in Sweden", every title already carries "Business Central" before "| Verdana", and "Verdana Consulting" should never be the public phrase. The legal position is unknown and must be settled before launch: the research did not identify the companies behind the "verdana … ab" completions, trademark status in classes 35 and 42 at PRV and EUIPO is unchecked, and whether Microsoft's rights in the typeface name constrain a consultancy that works with Microsoft products is a question for counsel, not for search data.

For icons, **Phosphor 2.1.10** is the only candidate that meets all four conditions of the brief: MIT licensing without attribution, a first-party React package, real light, regular and duotone weights, and a documented `@phosphor-icons/react/ssr` entry for Server Components ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/@phosphor-icons/react), [GitHub](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/react#react-server-components-and-ssr)). Its weakness is maintenance, with no npm release since 22 May 2025 and an icon set unchanged since March 2024 ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/@phosphor-icons/react)), which matters little for a fixed set of a few dozen MIT-licensed glyphs. Lucide, the earlier set, and Tabler are actively maintained but share a single-weight outline style that, in the icon research's design judgment, would not lift the site above a generic look; Solar requires CC BY 4.0 attribution ([Iconify](https://api.iconify.design/collection?prefix=solar&info=true)); and Untitled UI's bundled licence bars redistribution despite MIT metadata ([unpkg](https://unpkg.com/@untitledui/icons@0.0.23/LICENSE)). Use Light at 28–32 px for content, Regular at 16–24 px for interface elements and Duotone at 24 px only for the six services plus advisory, which keeps the rendered stroke between 1.25 and 1.5 px. Map implementation to `StackPlusIcon`, migration to `CloudArrowUpIcon`, integrations to `PlugsConnectedIcon`, support to `LifebuoyIcon`, management to `ShieldCheckIcon`, consultants to `UsersThreeIcon` and advisory to `CompassIcon`. Tabler 3.49.0 at stroke 1.5, or 1.25 from 28 px up, is the fallback if Phosphor stalls ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/@tabler/icons-react)). The build already lists Phosphor in `optimizePackageImports` ([next.config.ts](../../next.config.ts)).

Microsoft's own icons stay off the marketing pages except, optionally, inside a section titled as an integration architecture. Microsoft's Power Platform and Dynamics 365 icon pages state that "Microsoft permits the use of these icons in architectural diagrams, training materials, or documentation", and they forbid using the icons to represent your own product or service ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/guidance/icons), [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/get-started/icons)). The Fluent brand-icon CDN falls under the Fabric Assets License, which limits use to applications that integrate with Microsoft services and bars marketing-branding uses ([Microsoft](https://res-1.cdn.office.net/files/fabric/assets/microsoft_fabric_assets_license_agreement_nov_2019.pdf)), and Microsoft's trademark guidelines require a license for app icons and treat them as launchers rather than logos ([Microsoft](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/mscle/documents/presentations/FY27_Microsoft_365_trademark_guidelines.pdf), [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks)). The ecosystem section should therefore be a text-labelled diagram with a footnote naming the Microsoft marks as trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies. Phosphor's redrawn Microsoft glyphs, such as `MicrosoftExcelLogoIcon` and `MicrosoftTeamsLogoIcon`, stay unused ([unpkg](https://unpkg.com/@phosphor-icons/react@2.1.10/dist/ssr/index.es.js)), and no sparkle may stand for Copilot. The icon registry observed during this research maps its `copilot` key to `SparkleIcon` ([icons.tsx](../../src/components/icons.tsx)), which works only as a generic AI symbol beside the word Copilot, never as its logo.

## Thirteen weeks to NAV 2017's deadline set the publishing order

The order of work follows three constraints: nothing ranks while crawling is closed, NAV 2017's end of extended support in January 2027 is about thirteen weeks away, and decisions about slugs and the brand cost nothing before the first crawl but a great deal after it. Phase 0 therefore bundles every blocking or hard-to-reverse decision, Phase 1 publishes the converting pages and the time-bound NAV and price content in both languages before the deadline, and later phases add depth where competition is weaker or proof is needed. The windows for Phases 2 and 3 are recommendations derived from the evidence, not a measured schedule.

| Phase | Window | Work | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0, launch blockers | Before `SITE_INDEXING=on` | Choose the domain and host and set `SITE_URL`; freeze the brand string and the slug set; run the Bolagsverket, PRV/EUIPO and e-commerce act checks; finish per-language layouts and the single `pageMetadata` helper; complete the WebSite and Organization graph with real company facts; add Open Graph images; move fonts to `next/font`; confirm every route builds static; verify Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and keep "Search generative AI" on Include | Each item is cheap before the first crawl and costly after it |
| 1, money pages, NAV and price | Launch to December 2026 | 16 English and 16 Swedish pages: both homes, the Business Central overview, the services hub and all seven services, the insights hub, NAV end of life, the NAV guide and its Swedish pair, pricing, about and contact | NAV 2017 support ends in January 2027, and pricing is the most contested commercial query |
| 2, decision guides | January to March 2027 | Licences, partner choice, switching ERP, and the Power BI and AI service pages in both languages; the English-only localization and subsidiaries guides | Topical depth for buyers who have shortlisted Business Central, plus the English differentiators |

## Conclusion

The evidence turns SEO into a credibility engine rather than a traffic engine. When Swedish demand for every service term is too thin to measure, the pages that rank are the most specific and verifiable answers to a handful of high-intent questions, and those same pages, the price explainer that reconciles five competitor figures with Microsoft's list and the NAV guide organized by version, are the proof of competence that a firm without references otherwise lacks. The deadline logic also outlives NAV. Business Central on-premises versions lose support roughly every 18 months, with v26 ending on 13 October 2026 and v29 running to April 2028 ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/products/dynamics-365-business-central-onpremises-modern-policy)), and online customers must move to version 29.0 within an update window that closes in February 2027 ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/whatsnew/overview)), so lifecycle content is a recurring franchise rather than a one-off NAV campaign.

Consolidation is the other structural opening. As BrightCom, Navet and Sherpas sit inside larger groups and the Goodfellows and NaviPro brands disappear, their Business Central customers deal with new owners and priorities, and Google already completes "switch business central partner" with a Sweden variant ([Google Autocomplete](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=se&q=switch%20business%20central%20partner)). The link between the two is an inference rather than a measured flow, but it argues for giving the partner-choice guide a calm, non-disparaging section on switching partners, where an independent firm with a published method and support terms is the natural landing place. No amount of markup will make "Verdana" outrank a Microsoft typeface, so the trademark check decides whether "Verdana Business Central" can carry the brand at all, which is why it sits in Phase 0 rather than after launch.
