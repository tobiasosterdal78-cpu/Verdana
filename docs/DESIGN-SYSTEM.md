# Provisional design system — NOT YET APPROVED

- Identity: owner-provided original at public/brand/verdana-original.png. Preserve proportions and artwork. Compact header/footer framing retains both V and wordmark; hero shows the original prominently with background-edge blending.
- Navy #080F20; blue #3D6EFF; cyan #69C9EF; ink #152339; paper #F7F8FA.
- Font: locally bundled Manrope variable; display weight 500, body 400, labels 650.
- Layout: max-width 1280px, desktop side gutters 56px, mobile 22px.
- Typography: desktop hero up to 74px; section headings 43px; mobile headings 31px. Controlled negative tracking for display sizes.
- Shape: small corner radii, thin cool-gray borders, generous space. No fabricated logo or stock imagery.
- Components: dark navigation, original-logo hero, open benefits grid, service cards, ecosystem diagram, numbered process, contact disclosure.
- Motion: subtle hover movement and once-per-visit scroll reveal. Reduced motion disables transitions and smooth scrolling.
- Responsive: 4-column services become 2 then 1; process becomes 2 columns; mobile navigation is a toggleable disclosure with Escape support.
- Approval: original logo inspected and integrated; the overall design still awaits user approval.
