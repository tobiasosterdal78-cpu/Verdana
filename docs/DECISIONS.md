## 2026-10-11: Full bilingual site replaces the M0 preview
At Robin's direction the homepage preview became the complete site: home, services hub and seven service pages, Business Central explainer, insights hub and four articles, about, contact and five legal and trust pages, in English and Swedish. The research report (docs/reports/Verdana SEO and market research.md) set the structure, keywords and copy targets.

## Languages and URLs
English lives at `/` and is the `x-default`; Swedish lives under `/sv`. Each language has its own root layout, so `<html lang>` is correct without middleware, and `global-not-found` serves unmatched URLs. One route table (src/lib/routes.ts) feeds navigation, canonicals, hreflang, the sitemap and the language switcher, so a page's two URLs cannot disagree. The first Swedish addresses at the root (`/tjanster`, `/om-oss`, `/kontakt`, `/insikter`) redirect permanently to `/sv`. The Swedish NAV lifecycle article is `/sv/insikter/navision-end-of-life`, the phrase Swedes search for; the site was never published, so the earlier slug needs no redirect.

## Honest content

Microsoft list prices and NAV lifecycle dates in the insights are sourced and must be rechecked before publication. No Microsoft logos or badges. The only confirmed company fact is the organisation number 559602-6616 (footer and Organization data); legal name, VAT number, address and social profiles stay out until Robin supplies them.

## Brand
The owner's V is used unchanged (public/brand/README.md); the lockup and wordmark that came with it spell the former name and are archived (see Renamed to Vardena below). The header shows the V beside the name in type; further down the page only the V appears, so the name is not repeated. Icons and the share image come from the V, regenerated by scripts/brand-derivatives.mjs. The structured data carries "Vardena Business Central" as `alternateName`, to say which Vardena it is.

## Visual system
Light by default. The theme switch sets dark for the rest of the browsing session (sessionStorage), applied before first paint. Midnight bands (`.tone-night`) stay navy in both themes. Instrument Sans Variable is bundled locally; Phosphor is the icon set, the only candidate in the research that met the licence, React, weight and Server Component conditions. The Services and Business Central menus are Vardena's own design, not copied from Cryvanta; they open on click, never on hover. A short line under a navigation item and a blue tint on the current service icon mark where the reader is. Motion is CSS only and respects reduced motion.

## Header glass at fractional display scales
At scales such as 125 % and 150 %, the header's bottom edge falls inside a device pixel. Edge blending and the backdrop blur then let the page behind show through as a light line, or a dark one over a navy hero. The glass therefore lives on `.site-header::before`, reaching 1px past the edge. At the top of the page it is solid, in the exact colour the glass shows over the bare page (`--page-canvas`). Blur and transparency start only once the page has scrolled fully under the header (`data-stuck`). Re-run a pixel-row check at several scales after any header change.

## Language switch keeps the reader's place
The two root layouts make a language switch a full page load. Before leaving, the switch stores the top-level section under the header and the fraction into it in sessionStorage (src/lib/keep-place.ts). The new page restores that point with an inline script before first paint and again once web fonts are ready. If the section counts differ, the share of the page scrolled is used instead.

## SEO
Every page gets its title (60 characters at most), description (155 at most), self-canonical, reciprocal hreflang (`en`, `sv`, `x-default`), Open Graph and Twitter card with the share image from src/lib/seo.ts. Structured data: Organization, WebSite, Service, Article, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage and WebPage. Swedish copy uses the words people search: implementering, Navision, ERP-rådgivning, hyra konsulter. Crawling stays closed until `SITE_INDEXING=on`; `SITE_URL` sets the canonical origin.

## Contact delivery
Since 2026-10-12 every enquiry goes to hello@vardena.se, the address Robin confirmed. The server action (src/components/contact/actions.ts) validates the form and hands it to src/components/contact/deliver.ts, which sends it as an email through Microsoft Graph, from the mailbox to itself with the visitor as reply-to, and to the webhook too if one is configured. Graph rather than SMTP because the domain's mail is in Microsoft 365, where password-based SMTP is being retired. With nothing configured, or when sending fails, the form keeps the input and offers the same message as a `mailto:` link to the address. The address is also shown on the contact page and in the footer and is in the Organization data. Verified locally against a stand-in for Microsoft's endpoints; not yet against the real tenant. The paragraph below describes the validation and the webhook as first built.

A server action validates the form and drops bots with a honeypot and a timing trap. It posts JSON to `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_URL`, with an optional bearer `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_TOKEN`. Without a webhook the form says nothing was sent and keeps the input. At Robin's request the page carries no preview disclaimer.

## Home page: five areas instead of a NAV chapter (2026-10-12)
At Robin's direction the home page says less about Dynamics NAV and more about time and projects, warehouse, logistics and e-commerce, Power BI, and application management and support. Order: hero, services, Business Central overview, areas 01–03, reporting (04, midnight band), management and support (05), integrations, the NAV notice, process, principles, FAQ. NAV keeps its service page, guides and menu entries; on the home page it is one panel with the last three versions and their dates.

A check of Swedish Business Central partners (NAB, Exsitec, Fellowmind, BrightCom, InBiz, Modia, Evidi, Sherpas) found icon grids and stock photos, no readable dashboards, no flow visuals and no line between standard and add-on. So each area here has an illustration drawn in Vardena's own language (src/components/home/area-mocks.tsx), never a product screenshot or a Microsoft logo, and a note on what is standard in Essentials and what takes an add-on, a licence or an integration. Every figure in an illustration is example data and the caption says so; the illustrations are hidden from screen readers and the caption describes them. Claims stay within what Microsoft documents: projects, time sheets and warehouse management in Essentials, the Shopify connector and the Power BI apps in Business Central online, two major updates a year in April and October. No response times or SLA figures appear, because none are decided.

## Contact paths
A visitor should never be more than one tap from the contact page. The header button is visible at every width from 350px: "Contact" on phones, the full label from `sm`; below 350px there is no room beside the lockup and the menu carries it. Inside pages, `ContactRow` (the services-menu help sentence and the primary button) closes the process steps on the home and service pages, and `ContactPrompt` (a quiet question and link, copy in `site.contactPrompt`) sits by every FAQ, under the Business Central topic guide, in the article contents rail and at the end of legal pages. The site still shows no email address or phone number, because none is confirmed; the contact form is the only channel and needs `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_URL` to deliver.
