# Swedish ERP (affärssystem) market for SMB/mid-market and how Swedish B2B buyers choose ERP systems and consultancies (as of 2026)

Compiled 2026-10-11 for the Verdana website rebuild. Each finding notes the year of the data point and the type of source: [Official] = Microsoft, SCB or other authority; [Independent advisor] = ERP selection consultancy that does not sell ERP licences (it still sells its own services); [Press]; [Vendor] = ERP software vendor; [BC partner] / [Visma partner] = reseller or implementer (commercially biased); [Lead-gen site] = comparison or quote-request site with undisclosed ownership or funding. Most Swedish ERP content online is vendor or partner marketing. Neutral, quantitative Swedish data is scarce, and the gaps are flagged below.

## 1. Which ERP/finance systems dominate among Swedish SMBs and mid-market companies, and is there credible market-share data?

### Takeaway
No credible public measurement of total ERP market share exists for Sweden in 2025–2026. The market is segmented. Fortnox (600k+ customers) and Spiris (formerly Visma Spcs) dominate micro and small-company bookkeeping. Monitor ERP dominates Swedish manufacturing SMBs, with 42% in the 2024 Publikator survey and 46% in 2025, both as reported by Monitor. Microsoft (Business Central and F&SCM), Visma (Net/Business), IFS, SAP (Business One), Oracle NetSuite, Jeeves and Pyramid compete in SMB and mid-market, while Unit4 and Raindance hold the public sector.

### Cited Findings
**Overall market**
- A Swedish comparison guide states that "no public measurement" of total Swedish ERP market share exists. It lists only segment data and vendor-stated customer counts (2026) — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq) [Lead-gen site; ownership not disclosed; has a "För leverantörer" section and quote requests]
- Swedish ERP market size: about USD 471m / SEK 5.1bn (Statista 2025, as cited by the guide; the Statista original was not reviewed) — [Affärssystemguiden: Bästa affärssystemet 2026](https://affarssystemguiden.se/guide/basta-affarssystemet). An older Computer Sweden article (about 2019) cited IDC putting the Swedish ERP market at USD 750m for the previous year. The scope and year differ, so the two figures are not comparable — [Computer Sweden](https://computersweden.idg.se/2.2683/1.719084/de-ar-storst-pa-den-vaxande-svenska-mjukvarumarknaden) [Press; dated]
- About 50 qualifying ERP systems exist in Sweden, plus 20+ systems aimed at small businesses (HerbertNathan & Co, 2023, as cited by the guide) — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq)
- Swedish companies replace their ERP every 12–15 years, compared with every 5–7 years in the 1980s. A decade before 2019 Sweden had 50+ ERP vendors and about 100 partners, and about 10 new systems and about 15 new partners entered during that decade (2019 article) — [HerbertNathan & Co: Konkurrensen inom affärssystem leder till utslagning](https://herbertnathan.com/erp/konkurrensen-inom-affarssystem-leder-till-utslagning/) [Independent advisor]

**Vendor positions (HerbertNathan & Co, May 2023 overview of medium and large organisations; short commentary, not a survey)** — [HerbertNathan: Affärssystem i Skandinavien 2023](https://www.herbertnathan.com/sv/2023/05/12/affarssystem-i-skandinavien-2023/) [Independent advisor]
- Microsoft has a long-standing strong position in Scandinavia and its share is "growing gradually". Business Central and D365 F&SCM cover small to large companies across many industries.
- Visma has 30+ system types for micro to medium-sized companies. It is strongest in Sweden and Norway and its share is gradually rising.
- Monitor is "dominant in Sweden among smaller manufacturers" and growing among medium-sized ones.
- SAP leads large enterprises. It grew in SMB through Business One and ByDesign, mainly in Sweden and Norway. SAP plans to stop developing ByDesign.
- Oracle is active again, mainly in Sweden and Norway, through NetSuite, which is "steadily gaining share".
- IFS is gaining share in Scandinavia. Infor is struggling to hold its position. Jeeves' growth has slowed and it now focuses on Scandinavia. Unit4 focuses on service and project-based businesses and the public sector.

The guide notes that no precise share can be derived from these numbers — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq); [Affärssystemguiden: Bästa affärssystemet](https://affarssystemguiden.se/guide/basta-affarssystemet)

The bidder reached 90.5% ownership — [Affärsvärlden](https://www.affarsvarlden.se/artikel/budgivaren-ager-mer-an-90-av-aktierna-i-fortnox). The last trading day on Nasdaq Stockholm was 24 July 2025 — [Fortnox delisting notice (MFN)](https://storage.mfn.se/9fb78df0-609e-44cf-9208-94758957d998/avnotering-av-aktierna-i-fortnox-ab-publ-fran-nasdaq-stockholm.pdf) [Press/Official company notice]

Fortnox offers migration help from Spiris (Visma) Administration 200/500/1000/2000 — [Fortnox support](https://support.fortnox.se/byt-till-fortnox/instruktioner-per-system/byt-fran-visma-administration-till-fortnox) [Vendor]. **Manufacturing (the only segment with survey-based share data)**
- Monitor reports that Publikator's 2025 survey gives Monitor ERP 46% of the Swedish manufacturing market — [Monitor ERP (sv)](https://www.monitorerp.com/sv/kunskapsbank/aktuellt-fran-monitor/monitor-erp-starker-sin-position-som-marknadsledare-i-sverige-med-landets-mest-nojda-kunder/) [Vendor-published excerpt of a third-party survey]
- Monitor's undated English page reports an earlier Publikator survey of 1,800 manufacturing companies: Monitor 42%, Microsoft Dynamics AX/NAV 5%, Jeeves 5%, SAP 4%, Garp 2% — [Monitor ERP (en)](https://www.monitorerp.com/knowledge-base/latest-news-from-monitor/monitor-erp-has-sweden-s-most-satisfied-customers/) [Vendor]
- Publikator's own "Affärssystem NKI" document (October 2024) describes 1,822 completed telephone interviews with CEOs or production managers. **Public sector (outside Verdana's focus)**
- Unit4 and Raindance (CGI) dominate the public sector — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq)

**Size bands by system, as one guide groups them (no methodology given)**
- Small (1–50 employees): Fortnox, Odoo, Hogia. Medium (50–500): Dynamics 365, Visma Net, Monitor for manufacturing. Large (500+): SAP, IFS, Dynamics F&O — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq)

**Official statistics on ERP adoption**
- SCB's table on ERP use by size class covers only 2008–2014 and is listed among older tables that are no longer updated — [SCB Statistikdatabasen: Använder affärssystem (ERP)](https://www.statistikdatabasen.scb.se/pxweb/sv/ssd/START__NV__NV0116__NV0116Z/AERPSTL/) [Official]
- SCB 2025 figures for companies with 10+ employees: 72% buy cloud services, 34% have their own staff doing data analysis, and 35.0% used some AI technology, up about 10 percentage points on 2024 — [SCB: It-användning i företag](https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/forskning-och-det-digitala-samhallet/det-digitala-samhallet/it-anvandning-i-foretag/); [SCB: AI-användning i företag 2025 (PDF)](https://www.scb.se/contentassets/d2247e05fba24acd8669b366c58e399e/fd9901_2025a01_br_nvftbr2501.pdf) [Official]

### Inferences
- The site should not say "marknadsledande" or quote shares for Business Central in Sweden, because no credible number exists. Wherever the site refers to market data, it should give segment, source and year, e.g. "Monitor 46% inom tillverkning enligt Publikator 2025, återgivet av Monitor".
- The Swedish market has a clear ladder. Micro and small firms use Fortnox or Spiris. Growing firms move to a mid-market ERP such as Business Central, Visma Net, Monitor (manufacturing), NetSuite, SAP B1, Jeeves or Pyramid. Large firms use SAP, IFS or D365 F&SCM. Verdana's natural pitch sits at the step up from Fortnox/Spiris, and at the NAV installed base, which must move (see section 4).
- With companies replacing ERP only every 12–15 years, buyers treat the choice as a decade-long commitment. That raises the weight of trust, partner continuity and a documented "no lock-in" approach.
- Renames fragment keywords. "Visma eEkonomi" became Spiris "Bokföring & Fakturering", and "Visma Spcs" became "Spiris". Comparison and migration pages should mention both the old and new names.
- Unverified: Fortnox's private-equity ownership since 2025, with a stated rationale of heavy product investment and acquisitions, may push it upmarket. Verdana's "outgrowing Fortnox" pages should be monitored as that unfolds.

### Gaps
- There is no market share, customer count or installed-base figure for Business Central or Dynamics NAV in Sweden. None of my searches surfaced a Microsoft Sweden figure.
- I did not obtain Publikator's 2025 report itself, only Monitor's excerpts. Shares for Business Central/NAV, Visma, Pyramid and IFS in 2025 are therefore unknown.
- The Statista 2025 market-size figure was not verified, since the original is paywalled. Radar, IDC and Gartner Sweden ERP-specific reports were not found publicly.
- I could not verify Pyramid (Unikum) and Jeeves ownership or market position for 2025–2026.
- The Eurostat/SCB share of Swedish enterprises using ERP after 2014 was not found.
- I found no evidence that Spiris is discontinuing Visma Administration. Searches found no announced end-of-life, so it cannot be used as a switching trigger without new evidence.

## 2. Where does Business Central sit relative to these (size sweet spot, industries, strengths and weaknesses per credible neutral sources)?

### Takeaway
Swedish comparison guides place Business Central (BC) one step above Fortnox and below enterprise ERP. They describe it as a complete SMB ERP for about 5–500 employees (5–300 users) with built-in inventory, manufacturing in Premium, strong Microsoft 365 and Power BI integration, and the AppSource add-on ecosystem. The weaknesses they cite are a higher cost than small-company tools, CRM weaker than NetSuite's, and the fact that BC is not the specialist in Swedish manufacturing, where Monitor dominates. "Microsoft Dynamics" scores a low NPS among Swedish manufacturers in survey data that Monitor publishes. Globally, Microsoft reports 50,000+ BC customers (2025).

### Cited Findings
**Positioning in Swedish comparison content**
- BC targets small and medium businesses with 5–500 employees, and Fortnox targets 1–50. BC runs in the cloud or on-premise and Fortnox is cloud only. BC includes manufacturing (Premium) and built-in inventory, while Fortnox relies on third-party integrations for inventory. BC is a complete ERP covering finance, inventory, production and projects, with seamless Teams, Excel, Power BI and Outlook integration. The page's verdict: choose BC once inventory, manufacturing or deeper financial control is needed, and "many companies start with Fortnox and later migrate" (2026; undated page with no external sources) — [Affärssystemguiden: BC vs Fortnox](https://affarssystemguiden.se/jamfor/dynamics-365-business-central-vs-fortnox) [Lead-gen site]
- The same guide lists BC "for SMBs on Microsoft 365", from about SEK 765/user/month, and says it was a "Gartner Leader 2025 in 3 categories". The Gartner claim is not verified here — [Affärssystemguiden: Bästa affärssystemet](https://affarssystemguiden.se/guide/basta-affarssystemet) [Lead-gen site]
- Swedish BC buyer's guide: BC overall fits about 5–300 users (updated 2026-10-05) — [D365.SE (Moveahead AB / Dynamic Factory)](https://d365.se/businesscentral/) [BC partner-affiliated]
- HerbertNathan: BC and D365 F&SCM together cover small to large companies in many industries, and Microsoft's share is gradually growing in Scandinavia (2023) — [HerbertNathan & Co](https://www.herbertnathan.com/sv/2023/05/12/affarssystem-i-skandinavien-2023/) [Independent advisor]
- An outsourced finance and accounting firm uses Fortnox for smaller clients, BC for most clients and Workday for the largest international ones. It helps fast-growing clients move from Fortnox to BC — [Brain Accounting](https://brainaccounting.se/system) [Service firm; illustrative]

**Global scale (relevant for trust messaging)**
- Microsoft said BC passed 45,000 customers in April 2025, up from 40,000 in July 2024. At Directions EMEA 2025 it said 50,000, more than NetSuite's claimed "over 43,000" — [MSDynamicsWorld](https://msdynamicsworld.com/story/microsoft-updates-dynamics-365-business-central-customer-count-passing-netsuite) [Trade press]
- A blogger's tracker cites Microsoft saying 55,000+ BC online customers at Directions NA (28 April 2026). I did not confirm this from a primary Microsoft source — [yzhums.com](https://yzhums.com/63206/) [Community blog]

**Weaknesses cited by third parties**
- A Swedish comparison and review site says BC can be more expensive than some alternatives, especially for smaller companies, and that its CRM is not as robust as some others such as NetSuite — [businesswith.se: Fortnox vs BC](https://businesswith.se/jamfor-system/fortnox-vs-dynamics-365-business-central/) [Comparison/review site]
- A reviewer quoted on the site's BC vs Monitor page says setup took longer than estimated. It is unclear which product the review concerns — [businesswith.se: Monitor vs BC](https://businesswith.se/jamfor-system/monitor-erp-vs-dynamics-365-business-central/) [Weak evidence]
- A BC partner's blog test of ChatGPT listed "complex manufacturing processes" as a BC weakness. That is AI output, not user evidence — [Cosmo Consult](https://www.cosmoconsult.com/se/insights/blogg/vi-fraagade-chatgpt) [BC partner; weak]
- Manufacturing NPS in Publikator 2025, as published by Monitor: Monitor 40, IFS 0, M3 −10, Pyramid −14, SAP −26, Microsoft Dynamics −34, Garp −38, Jeeves −48 — [Monitor ERP (sv)](https://www.monitorerp.com/sv/kunskapsbank/aktuellt-fran-monitor/monitor-erp-starker-sin-position-som-marknadsledare-i-sverige-med-landets-mest-nojda-kunder/). In the earlier survey: Monitor G5 32, Monitor G4 24, Garp −12, Microsoft Dynamics AX/NAV −40, SAP −41, Jeeves −46, IFS −50 — [Monitor ERP (en)](https://www.monitorerp.com/knowledge-base/latest-news-from-monitor/monitor-erp-has-sweden-s-most-satisfied-customers/) [Vendor-published]

**NAV relationship**

**Prices for context (all excl. VAT)**

### Inferences
- **Credible sweet spot to state on the site:** roughly 10–250 employees (about 5–300 users). It fits companies that have outgrown bookkeeping-only tools and need inventory and order flow, purchasing, project accounting, multi-company and consolidation, deeper financial control, and Microsoft 365/Power BI integration. Best-fit industries to lead with are grossist/handel/distribution, e-handel, tjänste-/konsult- och projektbolag, and light or assembly manufacturing. Complex discrete manufacturing is Monitor's home turf in Sweden, so Verdana should not lead with "tillverkning" unless it can demonstrate depth.
- **The SEK price discrepancy looks like exchange-rate conversion, not old versus new prices.** Microsoft's SEK figures equal $80, $110 and $8 multiplied by about 9.56 (764.70/80 = 9.559; 1,051.40/110 = 9.558; 76.50/8 = 9.56). Navet's equal the same USD prices multiplied by about 11.05. So both reflect the October 2025 list, and Team Members appears unchanged at $8. If Verdana shows licence prices, it should use "från ca 765 kr/användare/mån (Microsofts listpris, exkl. moms, hämtat [datum])" and link to Microsoft.
- Low manufacturing NPS for "Microsoft Dynamics" likely mixes old AX/NAV installations with BC (inference). It supports the message that partner and implementation quality decide the outcome, not just the product. The figure itself should not be repeated on the site.
- Global customer counts (50,000+ per Microsoft, 2025) are safe trust anchors for the product, provided they are attributed to Microsoft. Verdana cannot borrow trust from its own references, but it can lean on product maturity and Microsoft's roadmap.

### Gaps
- I found no neutral Swedish evaluation of BC's strengths and weaknesses. Gartner, IDC and Forrester reports were not accessible, and the "Gartner Leader 2025" claim is unverified.
- There is no BC-specific (as opposed to AX/NAV) satisfaction data for Sweden.
- Not researched: native Swedish localisation scope, i.e. whether payroll, SIE, Peppol e-invoicing and Bankgirot files are included or need apps. This must be verified before it goes into comparison tables.
- There is no Swedish data on BC adoption by industry.

## 3. Typical buyer profiles (company size bands, industries, roles involved and what each cares about)

### Takeaway
Sweden has about 1.2 million companies, but only about 41,000 small (10–49 employees) and about 7,200 medium (50–249) companies (2023). That pool of roughly 48,000 is the realistic market for a BC consultancy. Swedish data on buying committees is thin. Surveys of manufacturers interview the VD or production manager. Finance-team surveys show manual work and lack of analysis time as the main pains. An independent advisor stresses process fit, usability, the vendor's delivery capacity, security and price. Role-specific concerns are largely inferred.

### Cited Findings
**Company size base**
- About 1.2 million companies exist in Sweden, and roughly three quarters are one-person companies — [Ekonomifakta: Företagare](https://www.ekonomifakta.se/sakomraden/foretagande/naringslivet/foretagare_1208907.html) [Business-funded statistics site]
- 96% of Swedish companies have 0–9 employees, 99.9% have 0–249, and 0.1% are large with 250+. The page was updated 2026-05-08; it does not name the table's source or reference year — [Ekonomifakta: Företag per storleksklass](https://www.ekonomifakta.se/sakomraden/foretagande/foretag/foretag-per-storleksklass_1212719.html)
- About 41,000 small companies (10–49 employees) and about 7,200 medium-sized companies (50–249) existed in 2023 — [Svenskt Näringsliv SME](https://www.svensktnaringsliv.se/sme/) [Employer organisation; secondary]
- SCB Företagens ekonomi 2024 (non-financial business sector): companies with 10–19 employees are 1.6% of all companies, 20–49 are 0.9%, 50–99 are 0.3% and 100–249 are 0.2%. Micro companies are 97.0% — [SCB: Företagens ekonomi 2024](https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/naringsverksamhet-och-utrikeshandel/foretagens-produktion-forsaljning-och-ekonomi/foretagens-ekonomi/pong/statistiknyhet/foretagens-ekonomi-2024) [Official]
- SCB published 2025 counts of companies by size class as xlsx on 2026-02-20; I did not open the file — [SCB: Aktuell statistik från Företagsregistret](https://www.scb.se/vara-tjanster/bestall-data-och-statistik/foretagsregistret/aktuell-statistik-fran-foretagsregistret/) [Official]

**Who answers and decides**
- Publikator's ERP survey of Swedish manufacturers interviewed CEOs or production managers: 1,822 telephone interviews in 2024 — [Publikator Affärssystem NKI (PDF)](https://www.publikator.se/v3/publikatornki.pdf) (via search summary; not locally verified)
- An independent advisor (April 2025) recommends the following:
  - Start from key questions: which processes the system must support, cloud or on-premise, international operations, the most critical processes, current costs and budget, and whether employees find the system easy to use.
  - Evaluate on functional requirements, usability, the vendor's delivery and development capacity, information/IT security and technical requirements, and price.
  - Expect about 3–5 realistic systems for a manufacturer or a public or service organisation.
  - Engage an independent advisor if internal expertise is lacking.

  Source: [HerbertNathan & Co: Konsten att välja rätt affärssystem](https://www.herbertnathan.com/sv/2025/04/09/konsten-att-valja-ratt-affarssystem/) [Independent advisor]

**Finance-team pain points (vendor-run surveys)**
- A Visma survey of Swedish finance departments found that a majority of staff spend at least one full workday a week on manual, repetitive tasks. Invoice handling is the biggest time thief, followed by closing. It was cited by a Visma reseller about 2 years ago, and the original report was not found — [WH Group](https://whgroup.se/artikel/har-du-rad-att-fortsatta-arbeta-manuellt-pa-ekonomiavdelningen/) [Visma partner]
- In a VIEW Group survey, 32% of companies say they spend too much time on ad hoc tasks and 51% lack time to provide insights and analysis. 61% expect to work in a cloud-based ERP, and 27% expect AI to take over manual tasks. The survey year is not stated — [VIEW Group](https://viewgroup.se/kunskap-och-insikt/framtidens-ekonomiavdelning/) [Finance consultancy]
- Reporting problems often start in fragmented data, differing definitions and manual flows. Excel files passed between group companies show different versions of the same KPI — [CFO Svepet](https://www.cfosvepet.se/artikel/har-ni-ett-rapporteringsproblem-eller-egentligen-ett-dataproblem) [Advisory content]

**Industry signals**
- Manufacturing: Monitor dominates (see section 1).
- E-commerce: a Swedish e-commerce agency publishes a 2025 guide to integrating with BC, a sign that web shops connect to BC — [Wallmander & Co](https://www.wallmanderco.se/knowledge-hub/integration-mot-business-central/) [Agency]
- Service and finance-outsourcing firms run most clients on BC — [Brain Accounting](https://brainaccounting.se/system)
- Cloud readiness: 72% of Swedish companies with 10+ employees buy cloud services (2025) — [SCB](https://www.scb.se/hitta-statistik/statistik-efter-amne/forskning-och-det-digitala-samhallet/det-digitala-samhallet/it-anvandning-i-foretag/) [Official]

### Inferences
- **Target sizes to state:** "för bolag med ca 10–250 anställda / 5–300 användare". Avoid micro firms, where Fortnox or Spiris is the rational choice, and say so openly. That honesty is a trust signal.
- **Role-to-message mapping (inferred, not survey-based):**
  - VD/ägare: growth without more admin staff, risk and total cost, time to value, partner continuity. Ten-plus-year decision.
  - CFO/ekonomichef: faster månadsbokslut, consolidation across bolag, reliable reporting and Power BI instead of Excel, less manual invoice handling, audit trail and attestflöden. The Visma and VIEW survey pains map directly here.
  - IT-chef: security and Microsoft 365/Entra ID integration, cloud operations, APIs and integrations, upgrade path without custom-code lock-in.
  - COO/logistikchef/inköp: inventory accuracy, order-to-delivery, purchasing, warehouse, EDI and e-commerce order flow.
  - Produktionschef: MRP and production orders. This is a competitive arena (Monitor), so Verdana needs specific proof here.
  - End users: usability. HerbertNathan explicitly lists "easy to use".
- **Suggested page structure:** separate landing pages per industry (grossist/handel, e-handel, tjänste-/projektbolag, lätt tillverkning) and per role (för ekonomichefen, för IT-chefen). The FAQ should answer each role's typical objection.

### Gaps
- I found no Swedish survey on ERP buying-committee composition, such as who initiates, who decides, or how many people are involved.
- No industry breakdown of BC adoption in Sweden.
- A search summary mentioned an Xledger survey of ekonomichefer and VDs in which 50% plan to evaluate their system within three years. I could not locate or verify the source page, so the figure is excluded from the findings.

## 4. Triggers to switch ERP in Sweden, and the most common fears and objections

### Takeaway
The most verifiable, time-bound trigger for Verdana is Microsoft's NAV end-of-support schedule. NAV 2016 extended support ended in April 2026. NAV 2017 ends in January 2027, three months from now. NAV 2018 ends in January 2028. ## 5. How Swedish buyers evaluate and select an ERP consultancy, and what a brand-new firm can credibly offer instead of references

### Takeaway
Swedish guidance converges on a consistent checklist. Most of it is authored by partners and vendors, with HerbertNathan and Radar as the more independent voices. The checklist: industry experience; meeting the people who will actually deliver, not the salespeople; talking to customers and references; a clear methodology including change management; support after go-live; standard first with no custom-code lock-in; the vendor's delivery capacity; and price as one criterion among several. A firm without references must replace social proof with verifiable people, transparent process and scope, and commercial terms that reduce the buyer's risk.

### Cited Findings
- Choose the partner as carefully as the system:
  - Failed projects often stem from poor communication or a partner that did not understand the business.
  - Talk to customers of several partners, and take time to meet several partners to find one you trust.
  - Check industry experience and how long the partner has implemented ERP.
  - Meet the intended project manager and consultants, not just the sales team, and ask about their methodology (agile or waterfall) and how they involve the customer.

  Source: [WH Group: Välj inte bara affärssystem, välj systempartner](https://whgroup.se/artikel/affarssystem/valj-inte-bara-affarssystem-valj-systempartner/) [Visma partner]
- Ask reference customers about the biggest unexpected challenge, how well the team understood the business, what they would do differently, and how support works when something critical breaks. Make reference visits to similar businesses. Prefer configurability over custom code to avoid lock-in — [Visma guide](https://www.visma-net.se/artiklar/guide-sa-gor-jamfor-du-olika-affarssystem-pa-basta-satt) [Vendor]
- The evaluation should cover methodology, change management and long-term support after go-live, not only technical system knowledge — [Implema: Välja partner för Dynamics 365-implementering](https://implema.se/insights/valja-dynamics-365-implementering-partner/) [Dynamics partner]
- The customer knows its processes and data best. A good partner wants the customer to take an active role and build internal competence — [Systemstöd](https://www.systemstod.se/tjanster/implementera-affarssystem) [ERP consultancy]; [Fellowmind: 5 vanliga misstag](https://www.fellowmind.com/sv-se/inspiration-och-nyheter/5-vanliga-misstag-vid-upphandlingen-av-ett-nytt-affarssystem-och-hur-du-undviker-dem/) [BC partner]
- A good partner is flexible with time estimates — [Xledger](https://xledger.com/se/kunskapsbank/artiklar/att-implementera-ett-nytt-affarssystem/) [Vendor]
- An independent advisor lists the vendor's "delivery and development capacity" as an evaluation criterion alongside function, usability, security and price. It recommends an independent advisor when buyers lack procurement experience (2025) — [HerbertNathan & Co](https://www.herbertnathan.com/sv/2025/04/09/konsten-att-valja-ratt-affarssystem/) [Independent advisor]
- In Radar's Leverantörskvalitet 2026, more than 1,300 Swedish organisations rated their IT suppliers. Supplier choice "is no longer only about price", and risk minimisation, value creation and the ability to act as a strategic partner matter. This is a secondary summary by a vendor, and the full report was not reviewed — [Cuebid on Radar Leverantörskvalitet 2026](https://cuebid.se/kunskapshub/radar-group-rapport-leverantorskvalitet-2026/) [Secondary]

**Commercial models observed**
- A Swedish BC partner's FAQ (updated 2025) says the price usually includes licences, cloud setup, adapting workflows, reports and integrations, data migration, training and initial support, followed by a support agreement. The price "kan justeras beroende på komplexitet", and there is no fixed-price statement — [Navet FAQ](https://navet.se/faq/vad-ingar-i-priset-for-business-central-implementering) [BC partner]
- A Swedish BC buyer's guide mentions a "starter package with pure standard from about SEK 50,000" (2026) — [D365.SE](https://d365.se/businesscentral/) [BC partner-affiliated]
- International partners advertise fixed-scope or fixed-price BC packages:
  - Velosio Express: $5,000 and 8 days, limited to one company, financials only, with no ISV add-ons or development — [Velosio](https://www.velosio.com/microsoft-dynamics-365-express-deployments/)
  - Fusion5 (Australia): fixed scope and price, 8–12 weeks — [Fusion5](https://www.fusion5.com/au/microsoft/business-central-rapid-implementation.md)
  - Levelshift QuickStart for companies leaving NAV or GP: about 6–8 weeks including 20 hours of training — [Levelshift](https://levelshift.com/services/microsoft-dynamics-365/quickstart-business-central-implementation)

  All are partners and none is Swedish.
- ERP consultant hourly rates in Sweden are SEK 1,200–1,600, per a guide that gives no source — [Affärssystemguiden FAQ](https://affarssystemguiden.se/faq) [Lead-gen site]

### Inferences
What a new firm can credibly offer instead of references. Each item mirrors a documented buyer criterion.
1. **Named senior people (the "meet the real team" criterion).** Give each a profile with photo, years of NAV/BC experience stated precisely, the industries they have worked in and the roles they held, a LinkedIn link, and Microsoft certifications only if they can be verified, e.g. via a Microsoft Learn transcript link. Promise in writing that "de du träffar är de som levererar".
2. **Published methodology (the methodology and change-management criterion).** Publish phases, deliverables, decision points, the customer's responsibilities and time commitment, a key-user model, the training plan and the go-live checklist. Include a sample timeline for a typical 10–30-user project.
3. **Risk-reducing entry offers.** Examples: a free 30–45-minute first consultation with a concrete output, and a fixed-price pre-study (förstudie, readiness assessment, or "NAV-uppgraderingsanalys") ending in a written scope, estimate and risk list that the buyer owns even if they choose another partner. This fits HerbertNathan's emphasis on preparation and the advice to "not skimp on pre-work".
4. **Transparent commercial model (the price criterion, Radar's "not only price").** Show the hourly rate band or a fixed price for defined scope, what drives cost, and how change requests are handled. Labelled third-party ranges can be used (section 6). In my searches, I found no Swedish partner advertising a clear fixed-price BC package beyond a "from SEK 50k" starter mention, which suggests a possible differentiation gap.
5. **"Standard först, ingen inlåsning" (lock-in fear).** Use extensions rather than code changes, deliver documentation, hand over admin rights, train an internal superuser, and make leaving easy. This turns the consultant-dependency objection into a selling point.
6. **Explicit support and förvaltning model (the post-go-live criterion).** State channels, response times, the named contact, Swedish-speaking support, and monthly or quarterly förvaltning options.
7. **Small first engagement as proof.** Offer a fixed-scope integration, a Power BI report pack, or a NAV health check, so the buyer can test chemistry and quality before a full project.
8. **Avoid unverifiable trust signals.** Do not show Microsoft partner badges, customer logos, project counts or testimonials until verified. If example scenarios are used, label them as illustrative ("Så här skulle vi angripa…"), never as cases.
9. **Neutral stance as a trust lever.** State when BC is not the right choice, e.g. micro firms on Fortnox or Spiris, or complex manufacturing where Monitor may fit better. An independent advisor is the buyer's alternative source of advice, so openly suggesting what an advisor would ask builds credibility.

### Gaps
- I found no independent Swedish survey that quantifies how much references, price, local presence, personal chemistry or methodology weigh in ERP partner selection.
- No data on how common fixed price is compared with time and materials in Swedish BC projects.
- Radar's full Leverantörskvalitet 2026 report was not reviewed, and I do not know whether it rates ERP partners.
